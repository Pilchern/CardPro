## 5. Final deal definition and config (`config/settings.json`)

- **Deal** = flag-eligible comp (exact or same_card; same card, grade, parallel, auto/relic/numbering) AND gross discount >= 30% AND **net >= max($15, 10% of value)**. Tier: **Strong** only from >= 3 SOLD comps; any ASKING comp caps at **Maybe**; no comp = no flag.
- **Net** = sale x (1 - haircut) - 14.25% FVF - $0.40 order fee ($0.30 <= $10) - ship-out ($0.82 Standard Envelope for raw sales < $20, else $6.00 tracked) - supplies ($0.50 raw / $1 slab) - buy cost (item + shipping, unknown = $4.50, + 10.25% tax). Haircut 20% on ASKING comps, 5% on SOLD.
- **Price band:** capital cap $150 all-in for deals (was a $50 ceiling). Below roughly a $29 sale nothing can net $15 (fact); the spread survives from about $60-75 of value up (fact, fee math).
- Justifications: FVF 13.25% on the tax-inclusive total (eBay help 4822, seen 2026-10-16) = 14.25% at the 7.53% average tax; Standard Envelope $0.82, < $20 only (eBay); haircut 20% = measured P25/median ask 0.79 across 44 buckets; $15 floor and $20/h are yours; $6 tracked and 10.25% Chicago tax are ASSUMPTIONS.
